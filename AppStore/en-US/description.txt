Real No-Limit Texas Hold'em, offline. Sit down with up to five AI opponents and play a tournament that ends when somebody owns every chip. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no internet.

A REAL POKER ENGINE
The rules are implemented, not approximated: correct minimum raises, incomplete all-ins, a side pot for every short stack, split pots, and the dead button and dead small blind as players bust out. The hand evaluator is cross-checked against a brute-force reference, so it will never miss your straight.

SIX OPPONENTS, FIVE SEATS
Each one has its own tightness, aggression, bluffing and trapping, and you pick how many of them you face:
• Viktor — sly and aggressive
• Rosa — patient, strikes hard
• Moose — calls everything
• Rex — total maniac
• Greta — ice-cold rock
• Ace — balanced pro
They estimate their equity by Monte Carlo simulation and read what your betting says about your range. On Hard they take the action at face value; on Easy they barely notice it.

BUILT FOR ONE HAND ON A TRAIN
The game saves itself between hands, so you can close the app mid-tournament and pick the same table up later. Leave in the middle of a hand and the chips you have already committed are forfeit — exactly as they would be if you walked away from a real table.

STATISTICS THAT ADD UP
Hands and games won, win rate, biggest single-hand profit, longest streak, showdowns won, and the best hand you have ever made.
