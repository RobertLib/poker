No account or sign-in is required, and there is nothing to unlock — tap Play,
pick a table and the whole game is available on the first launch.

PLAY MONEY ONLY. The chips are a score, not a currency. There is no wagering, no
cash-out, no way to buy or earn chips outside a hand, no advertising and no
in-app purchases of any kind. The app has no StoreKit code and no networking
code, so it cannot transact with anything. Because the game nonetheless simulates
poker, the age rating questionnaire is answered "Frequent" for Simulated
Gambling, which rates the app 18+; the separate Gambling declaration in App
Information is No, as no real money is involved.

Fully offline. All content is generated at runtime — the cards are drawn with
shapes and the sounds are synthesised at launch, so the app ships with no assets
and no network requests. Settings, statistics and a game in progress are stored
in UserDefaults on the device (declared in poker/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy, reason
CA92.1).

How to play a hand: on your turn the bar at the bottom offers Fold, Check/Call
and Raise. Raise opens a slider with pot-fraction shortcuts and All-in. The
tournament runs until one seat holds every chip; leaving the table mid-hand
forfeits the chips already committed and saves the rest, so Continue on the main
menu resumes the same table.

Localisation: English and Czech, switched by the device language.

The app supports iPhone (portrait) and iPad (portrait and landscape).
