# Privacy Policy — Ace High

*Last updated: August 27, 2026*

## In short

Ace High does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

## In detail

**We collect no data.** The app never asks for your name, email address, phone
number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required.

**It does not connect to the internet.** Ace High contains no networking code.
Everything — the cards, the sounds and the opponents — is generated inside the
app, and the game works entirely offline.

**No analytics, no advertising.** The app contains no ad networks, no analytics
frameworks and no third-party libraries that transmit anything anywhere.

**No in-app purchases.** The app contains no in-app purchases and no
subscriptions. The chips in the game are play money: nothing is wagered, nothing
pays out and nothing is for sale.

**Where your progress is stored.** Your settings, your statistics and the game
you have in progress are stored solely on your device using the system
UserDefaults store. This data never leaves the device. It is included in your
regular device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have one enabled, in which case
Apple's own policies apply. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.
Statistics can also be cleared at any time from the Statistics screen.

**Device access.** The app does not use the camera, microphone, location
services, contacts, health data or any other sensitive permission. It only uses
haptic feedback and audio playback.

**Age.** The game simulates poker with play-money chips, which the App Store
classifies as simulated gambling, so it is rated 18+. Because the app collects no
data, no data about anyone of any age is created.

## Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
